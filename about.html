Researcher & engineer

I'm a graduate student in Electrical Engineering at the University of Central Florida, focused on scientific machine learning for dynamical systems. My research focuses on physics-informed machine learning for dynamical systems, with a focus on system identification, uncertainty quantification, and optimal control.

Previously, I completed my M.S. at Northern Arizona University (Thesis: Physics-Informed ML for HVAC Systems) and my B.S. at Hanoi University of Science and Technology (Thesis: Fuzzy-Based Traction Control for Electric Vehicles).

Outside the lab, I run a Research Talk YouTube series for Vietnamese engineering researchers worldwide — 6,600+ subscribers and 30,000+ hours of watch time.
