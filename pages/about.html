I'm a Ph.D. student in Electrical Engineering at the University of Central Florida, working on physics-informed machine learning for robot control: system identification, uncertainty quantification, and optimal control.

Before UCF I completed my M.S. at Northern Arizona University, with a thesis on physics-informed ML for HVAC systems, and my B.S. at Hanoi University of Science and Technology, with a thesis on fuzzy-based traction control for electric vehicles.

Outside the lab I run Research Talk, a YouTube series for Vietnamese engineering researchers worldwide, with 6,600+ subscribers and 30,000+ hours of watch time.
